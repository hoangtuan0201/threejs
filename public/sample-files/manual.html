🔧 Installation Guide

  1. Location Selection: Choose a central location away from direct sunlight, heat sources, and drafts.
  2. Mounting: Use the included wall mount bracket. Ensure level installation for optimal sensor accuracy.
  3. Wiring: Connect 24V power supply and HVAC control wires according to the wiring diagram.
  4. Network Setup: Use the AirSmart app to connect to your Wi-Fi network.
  5. Calibration: Allow 24 hours for initial sensor calibration and learning.
Important Safety Information: Always turn off power at the breaker before installation. If you're not comfortable with electrical work, please hire a qualified HVAC technician.
